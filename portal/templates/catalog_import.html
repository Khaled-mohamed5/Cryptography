Accepted documents

Supplier feeds are treated as untrusted input. A document is rejected outright if it:

  • carries a document type declaration (<!DOCTYPE …>), an entity declaration, or a reference to an external document;
  • is larger than {{ '{:,}'.format(max_bytes) }} bytes, or holds more than {{ '{:,}'.format(max_records) }} <product> records;
  • is not well-formed XML, or is not rooted at <catalog>.

Individual records are skipped, and reported under problems, when they are missing <name> or <sku>, repeat an earlier SKU, or carry a field longer than {{ max_field }} characters.
